About WETLAND-DT

A multi-site platform concept for Ramsar and other wetlands, designed for GIS, hydrology, hydrochemistry, biodiversity monitoring and conservation decision support.

Current version: Mobile PWA prototype v1.0

Offline: Core screens and locally stored records work after first successful load when hosted over HTTPS.

Scientific integrity: Do not treat uploaded file names as analysed data. CRS, units, dates and measurements must be checked before modelling.
